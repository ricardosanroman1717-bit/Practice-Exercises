EXERCISE 6.3 - CHALLENGE QUESTIONS

1. Why is the mean useful when analyzing daily sales?
The mean shows the average amount sold each day. It helps the company understand its overall sales performance.

2. What is the difference between the mean and median?
The mean is the total of all sales divided by the number of days. The median is the middle value after the sales amounts are sorted.

3. Why might the standard deviation be useful to a business?
It shows how much sales change from day to day. The company can use it to see whether daily sales are consistent.

4. Which day generated the highest sales?
Thursday of Week 2 had the highest sales at $2,100.00.

5. How many days achieved the company's $1,500 sales target?
5 out of 10 days reached the target.

6. Which sales performance category had the most days?
Average had the most days, with 4 days.

7. If the company wants to increase its average daily sales to $1,700, what changes might it consider?
The company could offer promotions, suggest related products to customers, and study what worked well on the highest-sales days. It could also improve sales on slower days.
